This issue compounds over multiple rounds of interaction, severely limiting customization quality. In an attempt to address this challenge, we introduce a new self-supervised regularization, \ie, multi-round regularization, which is compatible with existing methods. Specifically, the multi-round regularization encourages the model to maintain consistency across different modification orders. It builds upon the observation that the modification order generally should not affect the final result. Different from traditional one-round generation, the mechanism underpinning the proposed method is the error amplification of initially minor inaccuracies in capturing intricate details.
